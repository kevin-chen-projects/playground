Scene 2 of 6 · Now meet the letters

The base pairs. Click each base to learn its rules.

DNA is two strands twisted into a helix. The strands are held together by base pairs — and the pairs always follow the same rule: A only pairs with T, and G only pairs with C.

You share more DNA than you'd think.

Because all life on Earth came from common ancestors, lots of basic "machinery code" — for things like cell parts, energy, and copying — is the same across species:

Wait — 60% of my DNA matches a banana?
