The big idea

From the 2022 Nature paper

Immune cells coordinate through a physical surface-protein network.

Shilts and colleagues systematically screened leukocyte surface proteins, measured direct binding, combined interactions with cell-expression data, and tested how selected proteins changed activation and cell contacts.
