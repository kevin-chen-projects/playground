STAR Prep
S
Situation
Sketch the context — when, where, who, what was at stake.
T
Task
What was your specific responsibility or the question you needed to resolve?
A
Action
What you specifically did. Use "I", not "we" — interviewers want to know your contribution.
R
Result
Concrete outcome + what you learned. Include numbers if you have them.
0:00
Tap the mic when you're ready. Aim for 90–120 seconds per answer.
