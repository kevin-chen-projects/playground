Hi Frank & Faye! 👋 This is your website **preview**. Everything you see — the
photos, the names, the prices, the reviews — is placeholder content we dropped in
so you could picture the finished site. **Nothing here is final.**

This document explains, in plain language:

1. The fastest way to tell us what to change.
2. How your **real photos** get added.
3. How your **videos** (highlight films) get added.
4. How your **real testimonials** get added.
5. What we still need from you to go live.

> **You do not have to edit anything yourself.** The easiest path is to send us
> your photos, videos, and notes, and we'll put it all in. The "for the brave"
> sections at the end show how to do it yourself if you ever want to.

---

## 1. The easy way: just tell us what to change

The simplest method — make a list. For each thing you want changed, tell us:

- **Which page** (Home, About, Packages, Testimonials, or Contact)
- **What it says now** (a few words so we can find it)
- **What it should say instead**

**Example:**
> *On the Packages page, "The Celebration" is $3,200 — please change it to $3,500.*
>
> *On the About page, our story should say we've been shooting for 12 years, not 10.*

Send that list back to us by email or text. That's it. ✅

## 2. Adding your real photos 📷

Right now every image is a gold placeholder. Here's where your photos will go.
**Just send us the photo files** and tell us which slot each one belongs in —
we'll handle placing and sizing them.

| Where it appears | What kind of photo works best | Orientation |

| **Home — big top banner** | Your single best, most dramatic shot | Wide (landscape) |
| **Home — "Luxury that feels effortless" side image** | A beautiful couple portrait | Tall (portrait) |

**Tips for the photos you send:**
- Bigger is better — send full-resolution JPGs; we'll resize them down.
- For the **wide banners**, landscape photos look best (they stretch across the
  screen). Avoid tall photos for those spots.
- Send as many portfolio options as you like; we'll help you curate the best set.

**How many to send for a first pass:** about **15–20 photos** covers the whole
site nicely (1 hero banner per page = 5, plus ~10–13 for the galleries and
portraits).

## 3. Adding your videos 🎬

The About page has a **"Wedding Films — Coming Soon"** section, ready for a
highlight reel. You have two easy options:

- **Best option:** Upload your film to **YouTube or Vimeo** (it can be "unlisted"
  so it only shows on your site, not publicly searchable) and send us the link.
  This keeps the website fast and is how most photographers do it.
- **Alternative:** Send us the actual video file (MP4). Works fine, but large
  video files can make the site slower to load, so we usually recommend the link
  method above.

Either way — just send the link or file and tell us "this is the main highlight
film," and we'll embed it.

## 4. Adding your real testimonials ⭐

The Testimonials page currently has **7 made-up reviews**. For each real review
you want to feature, send us:

- **The quote** (what the couple said)
- **The couple's names** (e.g. "Sarah & James") — or initials if they'd prefer
  privacy
- **A short detail** (optional) — e.g. "Red Rock elopement" or "June 2025"
- **A small photo of the couple** (optional) — a headshot-style crop is ideal.
  If you don't have one, we'll use a tasteful initials badge like the previews.

You can send **as many as you'd like** — we'll lay them out in the grid. A good
starting number is **6 to 9**.

There's also one large **featured quote** at the top of the page — tell us which
review you'd like in that spotlight spot.

## For the brave: doing it yourself (optional)

You absolutely don't need to do this — but if you're comfortable poking around:

**To swap a photo yourself:**
1. Put your new photo in the `assets/img/` folder.
2. Open the page's `.html` file in any text editor (even TextEdit/Notepad).
3. Find the matching filename — e.g. search for `hero.svg` for the home banner —
   and change it to your file's name, e.g. `hero.jpg`.
4. Save and re-open the page in your browser.

**To change text yourself:**
1. Open the `.html` file in a text editor.
2. Use Find (Cmd+F / Ctrl+F) to locate the words you want to change.
3. Type your new words in their place, save, and refresh the browser.

> ⚠️ Tip: make a copy of the folder first as a backup before editing, just in
> case. And when in doubt — just send the changes to us and we'll handle it.
