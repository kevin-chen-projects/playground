Our Story

Behind the lens, beside you at the altar.

Two storytellers, one shared belief: your wedding day deserves to be felt, not just photographed.

Hello, We're Suntastic

Faye & Frank

We met chasing light — Faye with a camera, Frank with a notebook full of vows he'd written for friends. A decade and hundreds of weddings later, we built the studio we always wished existed: one where the people photographing your day are the same people who help you say "I do."

Faye leads the lens with a documentary eye and a love for golden-hour portraits. Frank is our lead officiant, crafting ceremonies that sound like you — funny, tender, never canned. Together we keep the day calm, warm, and unmistakably yours.

We're proud to call Las Vegas home: a city where a chapel on the Strip and a silent stretch of red-rock desert are both just minutes away.

Our Approach

Quietly directed. Deeply felt.

We don't bark poses or stage stiff line-ups. We guide gently, then step back and let the real moments happen — and we're always ready when they do.

Fully Licensed

Officiants you can count on.

Frank and our associate officiants are credentialed to legally solemnize marriages throughout the State of Nevada. We'll walk you through the Clark County marriage license process, coordinate timing, and handle the paperwork so your vows are official the moment they're spoken.

Eloping, renewing vows, or hosting a full celebration — we tailor the ceremony to the moment and the venue, from a Strip rooftop to a desert overlook at dusk.
